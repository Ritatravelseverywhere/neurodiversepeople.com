About NeuroDiverse People

A free platform helping adults understand whether they might be neurodivergent — and what to do next.

Our mission

NeuroDiverse People exists because too many adults — particularly women — spend years or decades not understanding why everyday life feels harder for them than it appears to for everyone else. We believe that access to good-quality, evidence-based information about ADHD, autism, and AuDHD should be free, private, and available to everyone.

Why we built this

In the UK, the average waiting time for an NHS adult ADHD assessment can exceed two years in some areas. Autism assessments can take even longer. During that wait, most people have very limited access to information about their traits, what to expect, or how to advocate for themselves.

We built NeuroDiverse People to fill that gap — a free, evidence-based screening tool and resource hub that helps adults make sense of their experiences, understand whether a formal assessment is worth pursuing, and navigate the UK diagnosis system with confidence.

Our screening assessment uses original questions inspired by decades of published neurodevelopmental research, covering ADHD, autism, social camouflaging, and lifespan autistic experience. It is the only free UK tool that screens for all three profiles — ADHD, autism, and AuDHD — in a single adaptive assessment, and the only one that includes a dedicated masking section.

What we cover

NeuroDiverse People covers three neurodevelopmental conditions in depth:

  • ADHD (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder) — with particular focus on inattentive presentation, adult ADHD, and ADHD in women, which has historically been underdiagnosed and underresearched
  • Autism Spectrum Condition — covering social communication differences, sensory processing, masking and camouflaging, and the specific presentation of autism in women and late-diagnosed adults
  • AuDHD — co-occurring ADHD and autism, which research suggests affects 50–70% of autistic people and presents unique challenges that differ from either condition alone

We also cover the UK diagnosis pathway in depth — including NHS referral, Right to Choose, and private assessment options — because understanding how to access support is as important as understanding your traits.

How our screening tool works

The NeuroDiverse People screening assessment is an adaptive tool that adjusts based on your early answers. It covers four sections:

  • Attention & Focus — exploring attention regulation, impulsivity, and hyperactivity, inspired by WHO-published research into adult attention disorders
  • Social & Communication Style — exploring social communication differences and sensory processing, inspired by Cambridge University research
  • Social Strategies — measuring masking and camouflaging, inspired by UCL research on social camouflaging in neurodivergent adults
  • Life Experiences — an optional extended section covering social connection, language, sensory experience, and routine across the lifespan

All questions are original content written for this platform. The tool is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a reproduction of any published diagnostic instrument. It is not a substitute for formal clinical assessment.

Our commitment to accuracy

All articles published on NeuroDiverse People are written with reference to peer-reviewed research. Where we cite specific studies, we include full references. We are transparent about the limitations of the evidence base and avoid making claims that exceed what the research supports.

If you believe any content on this site contains inaccurate information, please contact us and we will review it promptly.
